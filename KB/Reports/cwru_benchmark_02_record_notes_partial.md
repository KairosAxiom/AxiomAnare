# CWRU benchmark — specific record notes (partial reference)

The following per-record observations are attributed to Smith & Randall (2015),
paraphrased in LynxEyes' own words. This list is PARTIAL: it covers only records
confirmed available to LynxEyes at time of writing. The complete per-record diagnostic
table exists in the source paper and should be ingested from a legitimate copy of the
paper to complete this reference (see gap note in chunk 4). Do not treat absence from
this list as "not diagnosable."

Records noted (drive end, 1797 rpm, 12 kHz unless stated):

- Record 209 (0.021" inner race): inner-race fault; envelope analysis of the raw signal
  yields a diagnosis.
- Record 222 (0.021" ball fault): the discrete components in the envelope spectrum were
  found to correspond to BPFO and BPFI (the latter with clear shaft-speed sidebands),
  rather than a clean BSF signature — illustrating why ball faults are frequently the
  hardest to diagnose in this set.
- Record 197 (0.014" outer race, centred): a partial diagnosis case; harmonics of BPFO
  are surrounded by cage-speed (FTF) sidebands, with cage-speed modulation the main
  identifiable characteristic.
- Record 235 (outer race): the time signal contains pulses at the fault frequency
  (1/BPFO) but subject to very impulsive shaft-speed modulation, making a clean
  frequency-domain diagnosis difficult.

General pattern: outer-race faults centred in the load zone tend to give the clearest
diagnoses of the group; inner-race faults are generally diagnosable via BPFI with
shaft-speed sidebands; ball faults are frequently among the hardest. The 0.014" inner
and outer race conditions are specifically cited elsewhere in the literature (citing this
benchmark) as difficult or not diagnosable.
