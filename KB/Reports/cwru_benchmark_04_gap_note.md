# CWRU benchmark reference — completeness gap and how to close it

Status: this reference set (chunks 1–4) is a house-authored summary of the FINDINGS of
Smith & Randall (2015), written in LynxEyes' own words. It is NOT a copy of the paper,
and it is NOT the complete per-record diagnostic table.

What is missing: the source paper contains a full table giving a diagnostic outcome
category for every CWRU record (fully diagnosable / partially diagnosable / not
diagnosable, by method). Only a handful of specific records were confirmable from
secondary sources at the time these chunks were written (see chunk 2). The remaining
per-record outcomes are not reproduced because the full paper was not available to
LynxEyes from a legitimate source at authoring time.

How to close the gap (recommended):
- Obtain a legitimate copy of the paper (institutional access, purchase, or author
  preprint). Full citation: Smith, W. A. and Randall, R. B. (2015), "Rolling element
  bearing diagnostics using the Case Western Reserve University data: a benchmark
  study," Mechanical Systems and Signal Processing, Elsevier.
- Extract the per-record outcome table from that copy and replace chunk 2 with the
  complete, sourced version.
- Until then, treat the acceptance mapping (chunk 3) as covering only the five labelled
  test files, and do not infer outcomes for records not listed.

Why this matters (A1 discipline): the benchmark is authoritative precisely because its
per-record outcomes are verifiable. Inventing or guessing outcomes for unlisted records
would reintroduce the exact "unverified reference cited as authoritative" failure mode
A1 exists to prevent — the same discipline applied to ISO clause notation. Leave the gap
visible rather than filling it with assumptions.

Governance: A1.
